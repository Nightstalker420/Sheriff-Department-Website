About Our Department

Integrity • Service • Community

The Richmond Metropolitan Sheriff's Office is a dedicated law-enforcement organization built around professionalism, teamwork, integrity, and community service.


Our members are expected to maintain the highest standards of conduct while working together to create an enjoyable, fair, and professional roleplay environment for everyone.
