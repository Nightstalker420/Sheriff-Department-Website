About RMSO

Committed to the community we serve.

The Richmond Metropolitan Sheriff's Office is a dedicated roleplay law enforcement organization focused on creating professional, realistic, and enjoyable experiences for our community.


Our members are expected to demonstrate professionalism, respect, accountability, and teamwork both on duty and within our community.
